# Project Concept

## Web app concept and unique features

**Memory Garden** is a personal diary web application where users can write about their thoughts, feelings, ideas and daily experiences. The main idea is to turn written notes into an interactive garden. Each note becomes a plant, with different types of plants representing different categories of notes. Their colours reflect positive or negative emotions, while the weather represents the user's general mood based on their recent entries.

## Examples of existing alternatives and inspirations

I found three applications with similar ideas:

- **[Voidpet Garden](https://voidpet.com/garden)** – combines emotional journaling with collecting creatures and growing a virtual garden. This is my main visual inspiration.
- **[Daylio](https://daylio.net/)** – allows users to track their mood and activities using a diary and statistics.
- **[How We Feel](https://howwefeel.org/)** – helps users identify emotions and understand emotional patterns over time.

Unlike these examples, I want individual written notes to become part of an explorable environment.

## Evidence of exploration and consideration of a range of ideas and approaches around this concept

A traditional diary could display notes in a timeline or calendar, while mood-tracking apps often use graphs. I want to explore a more creative approach where the user can interact with their memories through a visual landscape. I am also considering whether emotions should be selected manually or identified through text analysis.

## How it will use data

The application will store the user's notes, dates, categories and moods in a database. This data will determine the appearance of plants and the overall weather. Recent notes will have more influence on the weather, allowing the garden to change over time.

## How it might present a novel user experience

Instead of scrolling through text, users will explore their personal garden, interact with plants and open their previous notes. The garden will gradually grow as more entries are added. This could make journaling more interesting and help users visualise their emotional history.

## Identify the audience and how the web app aligns to their needs

The target audience is students and young adults who want a private space to express their thoughts but find traditional note-taking applications boring. Memory Garden aims to make personal reflection more engaging, visual and accessible.
